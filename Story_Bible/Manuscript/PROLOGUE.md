📚 NOVEL MANUSCRIPT: PROLOGUEThe planet does not cry out in a single, cataclysmic shriek; it bleeds in silence, drowning in the slow, rhythmic pulse of its own emerald lifeblood. Deep beneath the cracked, tectonic crust, the Lifestream surges—a glowing, subterranean ocean of ancient memories, unspent souls, and raw spiritual energy, straining against the cold iron conduits of a world being systematically hollowed out. Above, the sky remains a bruised, permanent gray, choked by the heavy industrial grease of an empire that treats existence as a resource to be factored, measured, and liquidated into pure corporate capital.The southern realm stretches out in a sweeping canvas of decaying timber and jagged stone, a desolate territory slowly swallowing itself under a canopy of sub-zero mountain mists. Here, gravity feels heavier. The air is thick, metallic, and biting, carrying the faint, sickly scent of distant industrial processing. It is a bleak, unforgiving landscape where fragile human souls are forced to grow hard just to avoid fracturing, forced to struggle blindly in the mud of their own desperate desires, manipulation, and the raw instinct for survival.Through the dense, swirling veil of the mountain peaks, the narrative narrows, descending like a heavy shadow into the suffocating isolation of a secluded mountain village. It is a cluster of fragile wooden cabins clinging to the uneven stone paths, wrapped in a perpetual chill that breeds a quiet, insular hostility toward the outside world. This is a psychological pressure cooker, a place where secrets are not kept, but buried so deep into the earth that they become part of the foundations.The focus shifts farther away from the village center, freezing onto the absolute fringes where the dark borders fade into the treacherous mountain paths. It halts before a rustic, weathering structure—an old barn standing in silent defiance against the mountain winds.Inside, the roaring chaos of the world outside is completely smothered. Shafts of weak, overcast light cut through the rotted wooden planks of the walls, illuminating thick, lazy dust motes dancing in the heavy air. The scent of dry hay, aged timber, and old mold creates a warm, deceptive sanctuary, entirely spared from the harsh concrete structures of the rising industry. In this quietest, most vulnerable corner of the continent, cradled by the soft rustle of cold straw and the lingering shadows of the timber loft, a young girl sits alone with her back against the worn wood, completely unaware of the shifting tides of the world outside.Here, in the heavy stillness of the dust and shadow, the story silently begins.
