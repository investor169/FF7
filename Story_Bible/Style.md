# ✍️ STYLE & VOICE COMPASS: FF7 - DARK THEME

## 1. Tone & Atmosphere
*   **Somber & Grounded:** The prose must remain heavy, realistic, and clinical when dissecting internal agony. Avoid flashy, anime-style hyperbolic descriptions.
*   **Claustrophobic Atmosphere:** Environments (such as the suffocating fog of Nibelheim or the dark interior of `The_Barn`) must possess their own eerie weight, actively pressing down on the characters' sanity.
*   **Imminent Dread:** Every scene should carry an underlying tension, making the reader feel that tragedy or systemic violence is always lurking just out of sight.

## 2. Pacing & Narrative Techniques
*   **Third-Person Limited POV:** Stick strictly to the limited, fragmented, or distorted perception of the focal character at that specific point in the timeline (e.g., Cloud's inferiority complex or Tifa's shifting mental states across 2000-2002).
*   **Visceral Diction:** Use short, sharp, and jagged sentences during moments of panic, dissociation, or trauma. Use longer, suffocating descriptions for heavy psychological buildup.
*   **Sensory Despair:** Focus heavily on grim sensory details—the metallic tang of blood, the bite of sub-zero mountain wind, the smell of rotting timber, and the sickly glow of Mako.

## 3. Dialogue & Subtext
*   **The Weight of Silence:** Characters should say very little but imply a massive emotional burden. True motives, fears, and traumas must be buried deep beneath the surface of regular conversation.
*   **Physical Deflection:** Rely on somatic cues (avoided eye contact, tremors, defensive posture, forced numbness) rather than loud, dramatic emotional outbursts to convey distress.

## 4. Execution of Explicit & Extreme Language
*   **Raw and Blunt Vocabulary:** When using extreme slang or explicit street/military terminology (e.g., "fuck", "dick", "cunt"), the words must feel earned, jarring, and realistic to the cold environment. They should reflect the corruption of Shinra, the rough nature of infantrymen, or a character's complete emotional desolation.
*   **The Contrast of Prose (Gritty vs. Poetic):** 
    *   **The Gritty Lens:** Use raw, direct, and unvarnished language during moments of pure systemic oppression, trauma, or detachment to maximize the impact of the tragedy.
    *   **The Poetic/Metaphorical Lens:** Utilize metaphor, synecdoche, and heavily atmospheric prose when the focus shifts to internal psychological shattering, profound loneliness, or the haunting, unspoken aftermath of a violation (Hidden NTR framework). This prevents the text from becoming cheap shock-value porn and elevates it into a serious psychological tragedy.
