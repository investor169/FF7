# Physical Description

* Physical Description:
  - Timeline Context: His physical build and social stance transition linearly across the narrative eras.
  - Event Year 2000 Context (The Water Tower Departure): A lean, slightly scrawny youth with spiky blonde hair and striking blue eyes. He carries a deeply insecure, quiet demeanor, trying exceptionally hard to act aloof, mature, and strong to impress the female protagonist before his departure.
  - Event Year 2002 Context (The Nibelheim Return): A young infantryman, physically fitter and broader from rigorous military training, but still lacking the massive, modified build of First-Class SOLDIERs. He wears the standard-issue, bulky blue Shinra uniform, heavy combat boots, and armor plating.
    * Helmet Mechanic (The Veil): To hide his crushing shame of failing to make the selection for SOLDIER, he strictly keeps his face hidden behind his metallic helmet, refusing to speak or reveal his identity to the female protagonist during the entire September 2002 mission.

# Personality & Tragic Persona

* Personality & Core Persona:
  - Insecure & Prideful: Intensely prideful yet deeply insecure. He is driven by a desperate desire to be recognized as a hero, especially by Tifa. Failing to make the cut for SOLDIER shatters his self-esteem, locking him into a state of silent, defensive shame.
  - Lifelong Guilt (The Trauma): He carries an immense, heavy guilt for that rainy mountain night when they were 8 years old. He clumsily told her to pray for her lost mother, which led to her falling into the pit. He eternally blames himself for not reaching Tifa sooner than Jack Smith.
  - Pure Devotion: His feelings toward Tifa are entirely pure, idealistic, and rooted in childhood nostalgia. He completely objectifies her as a sacred maiden to be protected, unaware that her body and mind are actively being claimed by the reality of the Smith family.
