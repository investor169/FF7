# 🏔️Atmosphere & Geography

* Atmosphere & Geography:  
  - Overview: Nestled in the misty foothills of the southern realm, Nibelheim is a modest yet thriving village of roughly 400 souls. Its heart pulses around a central cobbled square ringed by timber-and-stone houses with steeply pitched roofs and glowing paper lanterns that flicker even on the dreariest nights. Cobblestone lanes wind upward toward the wooden palisade, beyond which lies the dark, whispering forest known as the Whisperwood.  
  - Tone & Vibes: Warm, welcoming, and comfortable by day—yet transforming into a quietly uneasy, slightly mysterious, and fog-laden isolation by night. Shadows lengthen toward the forest, where fog rolls thick and the distant howl of wolves or the lowing of something that is not quite wolf can be heard. This crushing nighttime isolation makes it easy for dark, intense physical secrets and submissive training routines to be hidden entirely from the rest of the world.
